Hi, I’m Chaitanya.

I’m a 17-year-old A-level student in London interested in finance, markets and investment banking. I created The Market Loop to build my commercial awareness by following financial news each week and explaining it in a simple, useful way.

My aim is not to sound like a professional analyst. It is to show how I am learning: by tracking market stories, understanding companies, and connecting finance concepts to what is happening in the real world.

Issue 01

May 2026: The First Loop

Published May 2026 · The Market Loop · 4 min read

Welcome to The Market Loop — my weekly student-led finance newsletter where I break down markets, companies and financial concepts in a simple way.

This Week in Markets

This first issue is about setting the direction. Each week, I will look at the main financial stories that stood out to me and try to understand why they matter. Instead of just listing headlines, I want to focus on the link between news, investor behaviour and business decisions.

Deal / Company Story of the Week

Every issue will include one company or deal story. This could be a merger, an IPO, a major earnings update, a private equity move or a company changing strategy. The aim is to understand the business logic behind the event, not just the headline.

Finance Concept Explained

I will also explain one finance concept each week. Examples could include interest rates, bonds, valuation, discounted cash flow, IPOs, leverage, margins or M&A. The goal is to make each idea clear enough that someone my age could understand it without needing a finance degree.

My Take

Starting this newsletter should help me build commercial awareness consistently. I want to get better at reading financial news and asking better questions: why did this happen, who benefits, what are the risks, and what does it show about the wider economy?

Over time, I hope this becomes a useful record of how my understanding of finance develops week by week.
